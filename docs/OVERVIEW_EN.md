# Cyber risk and security control assessment

This GRC learning project assesses a fictional SME, Atlas Services. It connects assets and risk scenarios with findings, proposed security measures, owners and evidence needed to close an action.

## Deliverables

- 10 assets, 12 assessed risks and 12 proposed treatment actions.
- ISMS scope, risk methodology, security policy and management summary.
- A partial Statement of Applicability covering 20 controls and NIST CSF 2.0 Current/Target Profiles covering 13 subcategories.
- An Excel risk register with data validation, protected formulas and a dashboard.
- Separate personal-lab evidence: Keycloak roles/MFA/revocation, Windows 4625 correlation with Wazuh, SHA-256 restoration checks and a Git-history example.

## Scope

Company documents are simulated; lab observations are identified separately. T02 demonstrates one Windows event reaching Wazuh and generating an alert, but not 90-day retention. The restoration test covers three files, not a full service recovery. Proposed actions remain open and target scores are conditional.

ISO/IEC 27001, 27002 and 27005 (2022) and NIST CSF 2.0 are learning references. This partial case study is not an ISO certification or a completed professional ISMS audit. See [sources](../outputs/Portfolio_GRC_Anas/SOURCES.md).
